## 1. Personas

Names are illustrative.

### P-1 Agency Recruiter, "Jina" (primary)

|                                    |                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Role**                           | Senior recruiter at a small–mid recruiting agency (Team of Wakers' internal use is tenant #1), runs searches end to end |
| **Workload**                       | Several open positions across multiple clients at once; dozens of candidates per position at various stages             |
| **Tools today**                    | LinkedIn, email, phone, text, spreadsheets, Word/Google Docs, calendar                                                  |
| **Expertise**                      | Strong judgement on candidate fit, motivation and risk; knows clients personally                                        |
| **Wants**                          | Spend time on calls, assessment and clients, not on reading resumes and writing reports                                 |
| **Fears**                          | An AI that makes her look bad in front of a client; losing control of what is sent; more admin                          |
| **Quote (paraphrasing the brief)** | "I find, judge and recommend. I need help with the reading, sorting, comparing and writing."                            |

|           |                                                                                                                                       |

| **Wants** | See the pipeline across the team; balance load; KPIs (time-to-fill, conversion by source); keep candidate knowledge when people leave |
| **Fears** | Duplicate candidate contacts by two recruiters; inconsistent quality of submissions                                                   |

|                           |                                                                                                                                                    |

## 3. End-to-end journey (current vs. future)

| Stage                    | Recruiter does (always)                          | Today's pain                                          | With the workbench (AI assists on request)                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Client intake         | Talks with hiring manager, confirms requirements | Notes scattered; JD vague                             | Position record; AI structures JD into sections and Must/Preferred/Nice/Disqualifying; flags missing items; intake question list                                             |
| 2. JD enhancement        | Reviews and edits JD                             | Hard to benchmark                                     | AI market benchmark summary (on request) and improved JD draft                                                                                                               |
| 3. HM validation         | Gets JD approved; runs deeper intake             | Business reason, 6/12-month expectations not captured | HM approves the JD and answers intake questions **in the HM portal** (or recruiter records them); Candidate Success Profile created from confirmed data                      |
| 4. Sourcing              | Searches and selects candidates                  | Re-typing profiles; duplicates                        | Search criteria sheet; resume upload → structured profile draft; duplicate check; similar candidates in pool                                                                 |
| 5. Matching              | Judges fit, sets priority                        | Reading every resume against JD                       | Fit analysis vs JD and Success Profile: weighted score, strengths, gaps, things to confirm; adjustable weights; dashboard ordering                                           |
| 6. Screening             | Assesses and classifies                          | Judgement not recorded                                | Screening checklist; classification Priority / Potential / Hold / Not Qualified with notes                                                                                   |
| 7. Outreach              | Edits, sends, converses                          | Slow personal messages; missed follow-ups             | "Why you / why this role / why now" drafts per channel; 3-touch sequence; due reminders; **email sent from the app** with one click per message (other channels: copy + log) |
| 8. Qualification         | Calls; judges motivation and risk                | Notes unstructured                                    | Call question list; notes → structured fields (interest, comp, location, availability, counteroffer risk)                                                                    |
| 9. Resume prep           | Reviews tailored resume; gets candidate approval | Manual re-formatting                                  | Tailored resume draft (facts unchanged, original kept); **candidate approves or comments in the candidate portal**                                                           |
| 10. Submission           | Decides to submit; writes own assessment         | Report written from scratch                           | 14-section Submission Report draft; recruiter assessment section written by recruiter; **published to the HM portal** and/or PDF                                             |
| 11. Interview & feedback | Coordinates both sides                           | Status in email                                       | Interview rounds, status; **HM gives structured feedback in the portal**; candidate feedback; next actions                                                                   |
| 12. Offer & closing      | Negotiates, keeps candidate engaged              | Risk tracked mentally                                 | Offer checklist, counteroffer risk, resignation and start date tracking, reminders until start                                                                               |
| 13. Analytics & learning | Reviews performance                              | No data                                               | KPIs by stage and source; searchable history of feedback and outcomes                                                                                                        |

## 4. Pain points summary (ranked by likely impact, to confirm in interviews)

1. Writing: submission reports, tailored resumes, outreach (P5, P6, P4)
2. Reading and comparing resumes against the JD (P2)
3. Keeping track of follow-ups and pipeline status across many positions (P4, P7)
4. Vague requirements at intake causing wasted sourcing (P1)
5. Past candidates and client feedback not reusable (P3, P8)

## 5. Interview guide for validation (recruiters)

Also run short interviews with 2–3 agency owners (pricing, setup, data concerns) and 2 hiring managers (would they use a portal; what would make them stop).

1. Walk me through your last search from client call to start date. Where did the hours go?
2. How many positions do you run at once? What stops you taking more?
3. How do you track candidates and follow-ups today? Show me.
4. What do you send a hiring manager when you submit a candidate? How long does it take?
5. When would you trust an AI draft? When would you never use one?
6. How would you feel about a match score next to each candidate? What would make it useful or harmful?
7. How often do you reuse candidates from past searches? How do you find them?
8. Which outreach channels do you use, in what order? Do you send from your own email/LinkedIn?
9. What must never leave the system or be seen by another client?
10. What language do you work in with clients and candidates?
