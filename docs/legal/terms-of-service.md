## 1. The service

EaseMuse is a workbench for recruiting agencies: client and position records, job description structuring, a candidate pool, AI-assisted drafts (profiles, match analysis, outreach, qualification notes, tailored resumes, submission reports), documents, hiring-manager and candidate portals, tasks, analytics, and the administration around them. The service has two parts you should keep in mind: **you decide and the AI drafts** (section 4), and **the data is yours** (section 7).

1. A platform administrator at Team of Wakers creates your workspace and invites its first admin [or: you sign up online]. Sign-in uses an Interactor account; admins and recruiters must use multi-factor authentication [when we turn enforcement on].

3. You can invite hiring managers and candidates to portals. They are your contacts, not our customers; you are responsible for inviting the right people.

## 3. Your responsibilities

1. **Lawful use of personal data.** You are the controller of the candidate, client-contact and staff data you put in. You need a lawful basis and any notices and consents for it, and you must honour candidates' rights. The product helps (consent records, opt-out, export, deletion) but does not replace your duties. The [Data Processing Agreement](./data-processing-agreement.md) applies.
2. **Fair hiring.** Do not use the service to discriminate. Do not enter or ask for protected characteristics to decide on a candidate. You, not the software, make hiring and submission decisions.
3. **AI-in-hiring laws.** Some laws put duties on you as the employer or agency using automated tools (notices, bias audits, appeal rights). You are responsible for those duties; we provide documentation (DPA section 5).
4. **Acceptable use.** No unlawful, infringing or harmful content; no probing, scraping or overloading the service; no attempt to reach another workspace's data; no resale; no use to build a competing model from our outputs. Penetration testing needs our written agreement.
5. **Content.** You are responsible for what you upload and for having the right to use it (for example resumes and job descriptions, including third-party text).

## 4. AI features

1. AI runs **only when a user asks** and its output is a **draft** until a recruiter approves it. Downstream steps use approved content only. Resume facts are never changed by the AI.
2. AI can be wrong. Drafts, scores, reasons and fact checks are aids to your judgment. Match scores are a reference, explained with evidence, and never rank, hide or reject anyone by themselves.
3. AI use is metered and may be limited by your plan's monthly AI budget. When the budget is used up, AI drafting pauses and the rest of the product keeps working. We warn admins at 80% and 100%.
4. We use a third-party model provider (see the sub-processor list). We do not use your data to train models and require the provider not to [confirm].
