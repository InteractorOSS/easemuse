# Implementation notes (2026-10-07)

Choices made while building Release 1 where the brief or the planning documents left room. None changes a design principle of brief 1.1. The product owner delegated these to the implementation (2026-10-07: "You make those decisions"), so they stand as decided. Each is easy to change if counsel or a customer says otherwise.

## Decided

| # | Choice | Why | Where |
|---|--------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Only **Priority** and **Potential** candidates can move from "screened" to "contacting". Hold and Not Qualified cannot. | Planning handoff section 3 says only those two continue to outreach. | `pipeline/service.ts`, `screening/service.ts` |
| 2 | A **blind resume** leaves out the name and contact details. Employers, titles and dates stay. | Changing them would break "never alter resume facts" (brief 9.1). A stricter blind resume (employers hidden) needs a decision. | `ai/prompts/resume-tailor.ts` |
| 3 | The tailored resume's **facts are copied by code** from the confirmed profile (employers, titles, dates, degrees); the model only writes the summary, the bullets and the skill order. | Makes "do not change or exaggerate facts" structural, on top of the fact check. | `resume-tailor.ts` |
| 4 | The first interview round added to a **submitted** candidate moves them to "interviewing"; a round added to a candidate on hold puts them back in play. | Adding a round is already the recruiter's explicit act. | `interviews/service.ts` |
| 5 | When accepted placements reach the position's openings the position becomes **filled**; if one falls through (declined after acceptance, reneged) it goes back to **interviewing**. | Brief 12 and FR-OFF-03 say "filled when openings are met" but not what happens afterwards. | `offers/service.ts` |
| 6 | Offer **reminders**: one open task at a time (the day after the resignation date, a week before the start, the day after the start); completing one schedules the next. | FR-OFF-02 names the moments; the one-open-task rule of the task engine decides the mechanism. | `offers/reminders.ts` |
| 7 | **Privacy requests** get a 45-day deadline (the longest common U.S. state period) and a reminder a week before. Leads and admins record them; only an admin carries them out. | FR-PLT-08 asks for deadline tracking, not the period. Korea's PIPA is shorter (10 days). | `core/schemas/privacy.ts` |
| 8 | **Deletion** removes files, artifacts (all versions), documents, match analyses and embeddings of the candidate and redacts personal text in audit rows (activity details, approver names, free-text reasons, messages to the candidate, interview comments). Pipeline transitions and stages stay. A stored original that another candidate also uses is kept. | ADR-016, plus a decision on which free-text fields count as personal. | `privacy/anonymize.ts` |
| 9 | The opt-out link in an outreach email **suppresses the address and marks every matching candidate do-not-contact**. | FR-MSG-05 and FR-SRC-09 link them; marking the candidate also blocks drafting. | `messaging/unsubscribe.ts` |
| 10 | Outreach and report drafts use **placeholders** for names (`{{first_name}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`) filled by code at preview, copy and send. The report text says "the candidate"; the PDF header shows the name. | Keeps the candidate's name away from the model (FR-AI-09). | `outreach/service.ts`, `ai/prompts/report-draft.ts` |
| 11 | A submission needs an **up-to-date PDF of both the report and the resume**, and pins those exact files. Submitting the same candidate to the same client again asks for a reason. | US-802 and FR-SUB-04. | `submission/submit.ts` |
| 12 | The candidate's **approval of the resume is bound to the exact approved version**: approving a changed version creates a new record that has no approval yet. | FR-RES-03: an approval must not cover text the candidate did not see. | `artifacts/hooks.ts`, `resume/approval.ts` |
| 13 | The **client history and the pattern reports read only job-related fields**: the stages a candidate went through, the outreach answers, the hiring manager's ratings, and four candidate fields (level of the title, years of experience, skills, source). Never a name, contact detail, location, comment, or reason text. One loader does all the reading and a guard test (`facts.guard.test.ts`) fails if a forbidden field is ever selected there. | Brief 14, question 7 is about job-related characteristics only; NFR-AI04. Structural beats a filter. | `server/knowledge/facts.ts` |
| 14 | Any **value that names a protected characteristic is withheld** from a report, a history list and the facts of the summary, and counted ("1 value was left out"). The list covers age, gender, race and ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability and health, marital and family status and sexual orientation, in English and Korean, and errs towards leaving things out (for example "Asian markets" is withheld). | The values come from free text (skills, the agency's reason codes), which a recruiter may have typed anything into. | `core/knowledge/guardrails.ts` |
| 15 | **Small sample = fewer than 5 records.** Every row shows "n of m" and a "Few records" badge below that; a summary sentence that rests on such a fact is marked tentative. Years of experience are shown in four wide bands (0-2, 3-5, 6-10, 11+), not as numbers. | The brief asks for patterns that can be found again, not proof; wide bands do not single a person out. | `core/knowledge/guardrails.ts` |
| 16 | **Definitions used by the reports:** *put forward* = a submission exists or the candidate reached "submitted"; *interviewed* = reached "interviewing" or has a round that was not cancelled; *hired* = currently accepted or started (a candidate who reneged is not a hire); *quality candidate* (question 3) = the hiring manager wanted to interview them, counted out of those put forward; *response* (question 4) = the candidate answered the outreach, yes or no; *left* (question 5) = in a negative end state, at the stage they left from, with the reason code the recruiter recorded. | The brief names the questions but not the arithmetic. | `server/knowledge/facts.ts` |
| 17 | The reports show **only the positions the viewer can see** (the agency's visibility rule), for one client or for every client. Question 7 counts a match analysis only after the recruiter marked it reviewed. | FR-AI-02, ADR-006. | `positions/service.ts`, `knowledge/facts.ts` |
| 18 | **A18 gets numbered facts, not records.** The model sees counts and the labels of job-related groups, never a name, a comment or any free text from a record. It cites facts by id; code attaches the records behind each fact, so the links are always exact. | FR-KNW-03: every statement links to its records. | `ai/prompts/pattern-summarize.ts` |
| 19 | **The fact check of A18 is deterministic**, not the X1 model verifier: each sentence must cite real facts, every number in it must be one of the numbers of the facts it cites, and it must not mention a protected characteristic. A sentence that fails is removed (not repaired), and the draft says how many were removed. If nothing passes, no draft is saved. | The sources are structured counts, so a code check is stricter than a model. | `core/knowledge/summary-facts.ts` |
| 20 | **A18 is not streamed** to the screen. | A sentence that fails the check must never be shown, even for a moment. | `knowledge/summary.ts` |
| 21 | The A18 draft belongs to the **position** the recruiter asked from (artifact kind `pattern_summary`, subject position); it describes that position's client. | The kind was already registered for positions; no new subject type. | `core/artifacts/kinds.ts` |
| 22 | **The hiring manager's intake answers** wait in a `proposals` column on the intake record, one per topic, so an answer the recruiter already recorded is never overwritten. Accepting copies one into the intake as "from the hiring manager" and replaces that topic; the success profile (A5) uses accepted answers only. The questionnaire shows the recruiter's approved intake questions per topic (not the "gap in the JD" ones). | FR-HMP-03. | `positions/intake.ts`, `portal/publish.ts` |
| 23 | **Interview times:** the hiring manager offers 1 to 5 future times on a submitted candidate (one open offer at a time); the recruiter confirms one, which creates the round with the normal rules (a submitted candidate moves to interviewing), or declines all. A time that has passed since is refused. The default round name is "Interview round N". | FR-HMP-06, decision 4. | `portal/extras.ts`, `portal/resolve-extras.ts` |
| 24 | **A candidate's own updates:** a second submission while one is waiting replaces the fields it names. Accepting email, phone, compensation or work authorization makes a new approved version of the profile (the resume file is untouched) and so marks match analyses stale; availability goes to the candidate record. It needs a confirmed profile first (409 otherwise). The portal sign-in address does not change when an email is accepted. Activity log entries name the fields, never the values. | FR-CDP-04; profile fields come from the approved profile (T-2-08). | `portal/resolve-extras.ts` |

## Running things

- **Database roles:** `pnpm db:setup` now also creates `easemuse_datarights`, the only login that can change audit rows. The worker needs `DATABASE_URL_DATARIGHTS` for candidate deletion (see `.env.example`); the job refuses to run without it.
- **AI evaluation:** `pnpm eval -- --suite all --max-cost 15` runs the labelled data in `eval-data/` against the real model (needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; see `apps/web/evals/README.md`). Nothing has been run against a live model yet.
- **Print pages in a real browser:** see the header of `src/server/documents/render/live-render.db.test.ts`. The JD, resume and report pages have been printed with Chrome against a dev server and look right.
- **Not built:** SMTP transport (postponed on request), Korean translations (skeleton only, per ADR-015), a logo on documents, a Playwright happy path (the loop is covered by `src/server/happy-path.db.test.ts` at service level, and axe runs on the screens at component level).
