# Implementation notes (2026-10-07)

Choices made while building Release 1 where the brief or the planning documents left room. None changes a design principle of brief 1.1. The product owner delegated these to the implementation (2026-10-07: "You make those decisions"), so they stand as decided. Each is easy to change if counsel or a customer says otherwise.

## Decided

| # | Choice | Why | Where |
|---|--------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Only **Priority** and **Potential** candidates can move from "screened" to "contacting". Hold and Not Qualified cannot. | Planning handoff section 3 says only those two continue to outreach. | `pipeline/service.ts`, `screening/service.ts` |
| 2 | A **blind resume** leaves out the name and contact details. Employers, titles and dates stay. | Changing them would break "never alter resume facts" (brief 9.1). A stricter blind resume (employers hidden) needs a decision. | `ai/prompts/resume-tailor.ts` |
| 3 | The tailored resume's **facts are copied by code** from the confirmed profile (employers, titles, dates, degrees); the model only writes the summary, the bullets and the skill order. | Makes "do not change or exaggerate facts" structural, on top of the fact check. | `resume-tailor.ts` |
| 4 | The first interview round added to a **submitted** candidate moves them to "interviewing"; a round added to a candidate on hold puts them back in play. | Adding a round is already the recruiter's explicit act. | `interviews/service.ts` |
| 5 | When accepted placements reach the position's openings the position becomes **filled**; if one falls through (declined after acceptance, reneged) it goes back to **interviewing**. | Brief 12 and FR-OFF-03 say "filled when openings are met" but not what happens afterwards. | `offers/service.ts` |
| 6 | Offer **reminders**: one open task at a time (the day after the resignation date, a week before the start, the day after the start); completing one schedules the next. | FR-OFF-02 names the moments; the one-open-task rule of the task engine decides the mechanism. | `offers/reminders.ts` |
| 7 | **Privacy requests** get a 45-day deadline (the longest common U.S. state period) and a reminder a week before. Leads and admins record them; only an admin carries them out. | FR-PLT-08 asks for deadline tracking, not the period. Korea's PIPA is shorter (10 days). | `core/schemas/privacy.ts` |
| 8 | **Deletion** removes files, artifacts (all versions), documents, match analyses and embeddings of the candidate and redacts personal text in audit rows (activity details, approver names, free-text reasons, messages to the candidate, interview comments). Pipeline transitions and stages stay. A stored original that another candidate also uses is kept. | ADR-016, plus a decision on which free-text fields count as personal. | `privacy/anonymize.ts` |
| 9 | The opt-out link in an outreach email **suppresses the address and marks every matching candidate do-not-contact**. | FR-MSG-05 and FR-SRC-09 link them; marking the candidate also blocks drafting. | `messaging/unsubscribe.ts` |
| 10 | Outreach and report drafts use **placeholders** for names (`{{first_name}}`, `{{recruiter_name}}`) filled by code at preview, copy and send. The report text says "the candidate"; the PDF header shows the name. | Keeps the candidate's name away from the model (FR-AI-09). | `outreach/service.ts`, `ai/prompts/report-draft.ts` |
| 11 | A submission needs an **up-to-date PDF of both the report and the resume**, and pins those exact files. Submitting the same candidate to the same client again asks for a reason. | US-802 and FR-SUB-04. | `submission/submit.ts` |
| 12 | The candidate's **approval of the resume is bound to the exact approved version**: approving a changed version creates a new record that has no approval yet. | FR-RES-03: an approval must not cover text the candidate did not see. | `artifacts/hooks.ts`, `resume/approval.ts` |
| 13 | **Analytics cohort:** every rate KPI, the stage funnel and the source table are computed over the pipeline entries *added to a position* in the selected dates (both ends inclusive, UTC days; default the last 90 days). Time-to-Fill is the exception: it covers positions *filled* in the dates. Time-to-Interview covers entries in the cohort. | planning-handoff section 6 gives the formulas but not which records a date range selects. A cohort makes "last quarter's candidates" comparable and keeps ratios between 0 and 100%. | `analytics/kpis.ts` |
| 14 | An entry has **reached** a stage when it has a transition into that stage or a later one, so an overridden guard that skips a step does not break the funnel. A sent touch counts as contacted and a held interview as interviewing. "Has an interview" means at least one interview that was not cancelled. A recorded "no response" is not a reply. | The state machine allows skips (guard overrides) and the formulas count "reaching" a stage. | `analytics/kpis.ts` |
| 15 | **Who sees what:** every member can open Analytics. A recruiter always gets their own figures (the recruiter filter is ignored for them); a lead or admin sees the agency and may pick one recruiter. | Brief section 13: the recruiter checks "their own" performance; US-1101 says the recruiter filter is for leads. | `analytics/service.ts` |
| 16 | **Capacity:** positions per recruiter is the average, over the weeks of the range, of positions in sourcing, interviewing or offer (on hold, intake and draft excluded) at the end of each week, by the position's current owner. Time per position is the median of *active days* (days in those statuses) of positions filled in the range. A rate with no denominator shows "No data yet", never 0%. CSV cells starting with `= + - @` are prefixed so spreadsheets do not run them. | DP-5 asks for positions per recruiter and time per position; the handoff allows "active days" as the system proxy for recruiter hours. | `analytics/capacity.ts`, `analytics/csv.ts` |
| 17 | **Portal notices:** a portal user gets a short email when something is shared with them, when a shared item is replaced ("updated"), and when the recruiter answers their recommendation. The email says what kind of thing is waiting and links to the portal home; it has no content, no names and no sign-in token (they sign in again by emailed link if the session ended). The email is queued in the same transaction as the change and dispatched after it commits. A person who was turned off gets nothing. | US-1404 asks that the HM finds submissions without digging through email, not that the content travels by email; keeping content out limits what a misdirected message leaks. | `portal/notify.ts`, `messaging/templates.ts` |
| 18 | **Candidate status share:** the agency chooses `off`, `basic` (position and a general step) or `detailed` (adds the client, interview count and date of the last change); default `basic`. Only applications that were put forward to a client are listed. The candidate never sees client feedback, rejection reasons, scores or other candidates. A recruiter shares it once; after that the snapshot is refreshed in place on every pipeline move and interview change (no email each time), and withdrawn if the agency turns the level to `off`. | FR-CDP-05 asks for "a level the agency chooses". Refreshing in place keeps one share instead of a pile of copies; the recruiter's act of sharing covers the updates. | `portal/status-snapshot.ts`, `pipeline/service.ts` |
| 19 | **Portal adoption** = approvals (JD and tailored resume) + submission reviews + interview feedback done in a portal ÷ the same three kinds done by any route. A submission review is a hiring manager's answer to a submitted candidate: it counts as a portal review when the hiring manager proposed the decision in the portal, otherwise as recorded by the recruiter. | planning-handoff §6 defines it in one line. | `analytics/adoption.ts` |
| 20 | **A17 (feedback from a pasted email):** the email goes to the model without the candidate's name, email addresses and phone numbers (the same scrubbing now applies to A14 call notes). The model rates a criterion only when the text says how the person did on it; otherwise it is left empty. The draft is an `interview_feedback` artifact per round; **approving it is the recruiter's confirmation**, and it is refused until every rating that was given is ticked as confirmed. Approval records the feedback once per version (`InterviewFeedback.artifactVersionId`, new migration). | FR-IVW-04: "recruiter confirms each rating". One record per approved version keeps a re-approval from duplicating feedback. | `ai/prompts/feedback-structure.ts`, `interviews/feedback-draft.ts`, `artifacts/hooks.ts` |
| 21 | **Re-analysis (T-3-12):** an out-of-date analysis shows an "Analyze again" button on the analysis page, on its row of the dashboard, and as "Analyze out-of-date again (n)" for all of them (the owner, a lead or an admin; up to 50 at a time). A new analysis is a new unreviewed draft: the "reviewed" mark never carries over, so the recruiter reads it again before it feeds a call or a report. | FR-MAT-07 asks for the marker and a one-click refresh; the review rule is the brief's "AI output is a draft until approved". | `review-board.tsx`, `analysis-view.tsx` |
| 22 | **Tags and notes (T-2-13):** tags are short lowercase labels (at most 20, 40 characters each, no commas) that any member can set; the pool filters by them and a tag on the candidate page links to that filter. Notes are separate rows with author and time; anyone in the agency reads them, only the author edits, the author or an admin deletes. The timeline records that a note was added, never its text. Notes are in the candidate export and are deleted with the candidate (they are personal data). | FR-SRC-09 names tags and notes but not who may do what; notes can hold sensitive remarks, so the timeline keeps only the fact. | `candidates/notes.ts`, `CandidateNote` (new table, RLS) |

## Running things

- **Database roles:** `pnpm db:setup` now also creates `easemuse_datarights`, the only login that can change audit rows. The worker needs `DATABASE_URL_DATARIGHTS` for candidate deletion (see `.env.example`); the job refuses to run without it.
- **AI evaluation:** `pnpm eval -- --suite all --max-cost 15` runs the labelled data in `eval-data/` against the real model (needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; see `apps/web/evals/README.md`). Nothing has been run against a live model yet.
- **Print pages in a real browser:** see the header of `src/server/documents/render/live-render.db.test.ts`. The JD, resume and report pages have been printed with Chrome against a dev server and look right.
- **Not built:** SMTP transport (postponed on request), Korean translations (skeleton only, per ADR-015), a logo on documents, a Playwright happy path (the loop is covered by `src/server/happy-path.db.test.ts` at service level, and axe runs on the screens at component level).
