## About Me
Over the last fourteen years I have moved from an HR generalist on a retail floor to running global people operations for a software company that employs people in twenty-seven countries. What I enjoy most is the combination of the big picture and the small details: one week I am presenting a three-year operations roadmap to our executive team, the next I am sitting with an analyst tracing why a termination record did not flow from Workday to payroll in Poland. I believe good people operations is invisible when it works, and I measure my team on service levels, data accuracy and how few times an employee has to ask the same question twice.
